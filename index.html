Infinite Liquid Glass

An infinite, draggable, brick-staggered grid of rounded-rectangle glass tiles wrapped onto a very large sphere. Each tile is a thick refractive lens over its own video or photo, rendered with WebGPU through three.js TSL.

Drag to scroll: the grid wraps modularly, so scrolling never runs out. Fling it and the camera dollies back in proportion to drag speed, then settles as the grid comes to rest. Hover a tile to lift it, and hover near its edge to tilt that edge. A mouse wheel or two-finger scroll moves the grid the same way a drag does.

Every tile shares the same sphere, so the tiles join into one continuous shell: tiles at the centre stay nearly undistorted while those at the edges tilt away and lens hard. The bevel follows a Lamé curve with a flat plateau and a fast rounded shoulder, and per-channel dispersion splits light into colour along each rim.

The source is MIT licensed on GitHub at codyhxyz/infinite-liquid-glass. The effect design is inspired by Shader Development Studio.
